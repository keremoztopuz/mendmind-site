Privacy Policy

Effective October 14, 2026

MendMind does not collect any data. Nothing you do in the app is sent to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your iPhone

The apps and websites you pick, your answers at the gate, your tasks, your settings and your daily summary are all stored on your iPhone. They are shared only between MendMind and its own Screen Time extensions, through an App Group container that other apps cannot read.

Screen Time

MendMind uses Apple's Screen Time framework (Family Controls, Managed Settings and Device Activity). Apple gives the app opaque tokens instead of app names, and per-app usage can only be shown inside Apple's own report view. MendMind cannot read that data or send it anywhere.

No servers, no tracking

MendMind has no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no crash-reporting SDK. It does not track you across apps or websites.

Deleting your data

Open MendMind and go to Settings → Your data → Delete everything, or delete the app. Either way, everything MendMind stored on your iPhone is removed and the apps you guarded open normally again.

Children

MendMind is meant for adults managing their own phone use. It collects no data from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, for example if an optional, opt-in feature ever shares data, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date before the change ships.
