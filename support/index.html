Support

Answers to common questions. If yours isn't here, write to us.

How do I change which apps have a pause?

Open MendMind, go to Settings and pick the apps and websites again. Changes apply right away.

I tapped “Answer” but nothing happened.

The shield can't open MendMind directly, so the question arrives as a notification. Check Settings → Notifications → MendMind on your iPhone and allow notifications. If you'd rather not get notifications, MendMind's setup guide shows how to add a two-step Shortcuts automation instead.

How do I turn MendMind off?

To pause it for a while, use Pause on MendMind's home screen. To turn it off completely, go to iPhone Settings → Screen Time → Apps with Screen Time Access and switch off MendMind, or delete the app. Guarded apps then open normally.

How do I delete my data?

In MendMind, go to Settings → Your data → Delete everything, or delete the app. All data lives only on your iPhone, so there is nothing to delete anywhere else. See the Privacy Policy.

Some apps stopped being guarded.

iOS sometimes resets the app list after an update or a restore. MendMind will ask you to pick the apps again when that happens.
